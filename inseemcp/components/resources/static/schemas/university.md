# Universities Data Dictionary

This resource describes the various data fields associated with universities, providing definitions and explanations for each column.

## Definitions

**HAL**
The HAL (Hyper Articles en Ligne) identifier of the university.

**RNA**
RNA (Répertoire National des Associations) number identifying the association.

**code_rna**
Code RNA (Répertoire National des Associations) identifying the association.

**Pays**
The country where the university is located.

**mooc**
Indicates whether the university offers MOOCs (Massive Open Online Courses).

**scanr**
The official ScanR URL of the university.

**sigle**
The official abbreviation or acronym of the university.

**siren**
The SIREN number of the university.

**siret**
The SIRET number of the university.

**Adresse**
The official address of the university.

**Commune**
The commune where the university is located.

**Région**
The region where the university is located.

**article**
The article associated with the university.

**Lieu dit**
The specific locality or hamlet where the university is located.

**flux_rss**
The RSS feed URL of the university.

**inscrits**
The number of students enrolled at the university.

**libellé**
The label or description of the enrollment data.

**Académie**
The academic district where the university is located.

**commune**
The commune where the university is located.

**Localité**
The locality where the university is located.

**nom court**
The short name or abbreviation of the university.

**Code postal**
The postal code of the university's location.


**Code commune**
The INSEE code of the commune where the university is located.

**Code région**
The code of the region where the university is located.

**Département**
The department where the university is located.

**Elément ROR**
The ROR (Research Organization Registry) identifier of the university.

**English name**
The English name of the university.


**compte_vimeo**
The university's Vimeo account.

**Boite postale**
The university's postal box.


**Elément ISNI**
The ISNI (International Standard Name Identifier) of the university.
The URL of the university's ISNI page. 

**compte_flickr**
The university's Flickr account.

**compte_github**
The university's GitHub account.

**compte_scribd**
The university's Scribd account.

**compte_tumblr**
The university's Tumblr account.

**date_creation**
The date when the university was established.

**site internet**
The official website URL of the university.

**Code académie**
The code of the academic district where the university is located.

**Unité urbaine**
The urban unit where the university is located.

**compte_scoopit**
The university's Scoop.it account.

**compte_twitter**
The university's Twitter account.

**compte_youtube**
The university's YouTube account.

**Identifiant PIC**
The university's PIC (Partner Identification Code) identifier.

**Identifiant ROR**
The university's ROR (Research Organization Registry) identifier.
    
**champ_recherche**
The research field or area of focus of the university.

**compte_facebook**
The university's Facebook account.

**compte_linkedin**
The university's LinkedIn account.

**uo_lib_officiel**
The official name of the university.

**Ancienne région**
The former region where the university was located.

**Identifiant ETER**
The university's ETER identifier (European Tertiary Education Register).

**Identifiant ISNI**
The university's ISNI (International Standard Name Identifier).

**Statut juridique**
The legal status of the university. One of:
* Association loi de 1901
* Établissement Public à Caractère Scientifique, Culturel et Professionnel
* Autre établissement public national d'enseignement supérieur
* Établissement Public Local de Coopération
* Établissement Public Administratif
* Sans personnalité juridique - secteur privé
* SA à conseil d'administration

**compte_instagram**
The university's Instagram account.

**compte_pinterest**
The university's Pinterest account.

**géolocalisation**
The geographical location of the university, typically represented by latitude and longitude coordinates.

**Code département**
The code of the department where the university is located.

**Elément wikidata**
The Wikidata element of the university.

**anciens codes uai**
The former UAI codes of the university.

**identifiant_idref**
The university's IdRef identifier.

**uai - identifiant**
The university's UAI (Unique Academic Identifier).

**Identifiant OrgRef**  
The university's OrgRef identifier.

**compte_dailymotion**
The university's Dailymotion account.

**Ancien code région**
The former code of the region where the university is located.

**Code unité urbaine**
The code of the urban unit where the university is located. An urban unit is a continuous built-up area with a population of at least 2,000 inhabitants.

**identifiant interne**
Internal identifier for the university.

**vague contractuelle**
The contractual wave of the university.

**Identifiant wikidata**
The university's Wikidata identifier.

**Mention distribution**
The distribution of mentions related to the university.

**Elément Funding Data**    
The university's Funding Data element.

**compte_france_culture**
The university's France Culture account.

**texte_de_ref_creation**
The reference text for the creation of the university.

**type d'établissement**
The type of the university establishment. Either `Université` or `École`

**Numéro de téléphone**
The university's phone number.

**Statut juridique court**
The short legal status of the university. One of: `Association loi de 1901`, `EPSCP`, `Autre établissement public national d'enseignement supérieur`, `EPLC`, `EPA`, `Sans personnalité juridique - secteur privé`, `SA à conseil d'administration`

**universites_fusionnees**
The universities that have merged to form the current university.

**Site internet en anglais**
The university's website in English.

**Site internet en chinois**
The university's website in Chinese.

**Site internet en italien**
The university's website in Italian.

**secteur d'établissement**
The sector of the university establishment. Either `Public` or `Privé`.

**Site internet en allemand**
The university's website in German.

**Site internet en espagnol**
The university's website in Spanish.
