# SIRET

## Definition

A SIRET identifies a specific establishment in the French enterprise
registry.

## Format

A SIRET normally contains 14 digits.

The first 9 digits identify the associated legal unit.

The final 5 digits identify the establishment.

## Tool selection

Use a SIRET lookup when the user provides a specific SIRET.

Do not use a SIREN lookup to identify a specific establishment.
