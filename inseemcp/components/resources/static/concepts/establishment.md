# Establishment

## Definition 

A geographically individualized production unit that is legally dependent on a [legal unit](./legal-unit.md).

 The same company (such as a chain of bakeries) may have a single head office but multiple establishments spread across different locations.

### Main caracteristics

* Geograpgic location: specific place where the activity takes place (a factory, a store, an office, a bakery).
* Lack of legal personality: It does not have its own legal existence and depends on a company or legal entity.
* Identification: Each establishment is assigned a unique number, the SIRET (composed of the company's 9-digit SIREN number followed by a 5-digit NIC).
